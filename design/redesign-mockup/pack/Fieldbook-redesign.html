The audit

What is wrong today, ranked

This comes from three places: reading the app's own code, Astro's 14-page review, and running real questions through today's search. Size is S for a small change, M for a screen plus its storage, L for a whole new part.

Check first: the screenshot with a pin called Home

The public screenshot docs/screenshots/my-place-record.png shows coordinates next to a record named Home. I opened it. The pin sits in rural Kansas, close to the geographic center of the lower 48, which is where test pins usually land. It is not in Texas. Swap it for a record with no pin anyway, because a stranger reading "Home" plus numbers will assume it is real.

Search today, on the real manuals

Today a result only counts if every word you typed appears letter for letter, anywhere inside any word. That one rule causes most of the misses below.

Where I went a different way from Astro

  • Astro's Home hides the situations behind "Choose a situation". I kept the six most likely ones on Home, so Power out is still one click.
  • Astro kept five menu rows that look alike. Here each tab has its own color, a number key, and a short list of what is inside when you open it.
  • Astro's search is a results page. Here it is a pop-up that fixes typos, knows nicknames like CO, and works with the arrow keys.
  • Astro's headings use a bookish serif. I used road-sign lettering and the plain typeface US agencies use. Both are free to ship inside the app, so nothing loads from the internet.
