Something happening?

Fire, flood, injury, power out. Step by step.

Learn

First aid, water, shelter, food. Short versions of the app's guides.

Tools

CPR beat, tourniquet timer, bleach-per-gallon calculator, SOS on the light and buzzer, weather sensor reader.

Radio

Frequency sheet for NOAA, GMRS, FRS and ham. The Flipper can show them but can't tune NOAA.

Location

Your coordinates, plus an arrow and distance to water, shelter or a hospital. Needs the GPS add-on.

Never on it

Your plan, your records, your home details. The Flipper is safe to lose or lend.
