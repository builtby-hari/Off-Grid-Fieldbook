⚠ Radiation or chemical release, or told to shelter in place?
Get inside.The nearest solid building. Go to the basement or the middle of the building, away from windows.
Stay inside.Close windows and doors. Turn off fans, AC and heat that pull in outside air. Stay put until officials say it's safe, usually at least 24 hours.
Stay tuned.NOAA weather radio, local AM/FM or TV. Follow official instructions over anything saved in this app.

Source: CDC "Get Inside, Stay Inside, Stay Tuned" (radiation emergencies). If you were outside: remove outer clothing, bag it, and shower or wipe exposed skin.
