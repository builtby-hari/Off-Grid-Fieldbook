Get your state's map

Pick a state and the app downloads its map for you. After that the map works with no internet. This is the only screen that goes online, and only when you press Download.

One thing first. The download needs Apple's free developer tools on this Mac. Press the button and a window from Apple will open. Say yes, wait for it to finish (a few minutes), then press Check again.
This copy of the app does not include the map downloader. Rebuild it from the latest code.
